Our Soweto Story

Established on 9 March 2021 in Chiawelo, Soweto, Joss Sweet Crumbs Bakery grew from a local home-based baking initiative to a beloved community establishment located at 2526 Chiawelo on Chris Hani Road.

Our Mission

To provide the Soweto community with freshly baked and affordable baked goods every day from 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM with warm, local hospitality.

Our Vision

To be Soweto’s preferred choice for birthday cakes, daily bread, and community event catering.
