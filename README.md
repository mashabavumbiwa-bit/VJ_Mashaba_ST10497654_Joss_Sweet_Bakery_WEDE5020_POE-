## Project Overview
This project comprises the foundation phase of a 5-page responsive website created for Joss Sweet Crumbs Bakery, established on 9 March 2021 and operating at 2526 Chiawelo, Chris Hani Road, Soweto (1818).

Slogan: "Soweto’s Daily Slice of Happiness — Freshly Baked Since 2021!"

---

## Website Goals & Key Features
 Showcase fresh bread, birthday cakes, scones, muffins, and cupcakes.
 Resolve morning store queue congestion by providing digital enquiry and advance booking options.
 Provide an interactive photo gallery highlighting custom birthday cake designs and catering platters.
 Display store operational hours (5:00 AM – 10:00 PM), location map, and interactive order form.

---

## File and Folder Structure
 `index.html`: Homepage featuring hero section, slogan, hours, and daily specials.
 `about.html`: Bakery origins in Chiawelo (founded 9 March 2021), mission, and vision.
 `products.html`: Combined menu (Bread, Birthday Cakes, Scones, Muffins) & commercial services.
 `gallery.html`: Visual photo showcase of birthday cakes, fresh bread, scones, and cupcakes.
 `contact.html`: Store details, physical location map, and interactive order enquiry form.
 `css/styles.css`: Stylesheet (To be completed in Part 2).
 `js/main.js`: Interactivity script (To be completed in Part 3).
